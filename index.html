Heroes III SaveEditor — Heroes 3 save game editor online

A free save editor for Heroes of Might and Magic III (HoMM3) that runs entirely in the browser. Open a .GM1–.GM8 or .CGM save, change it and write it back. The file never leaves your computer.

  • Heroes: primary skills, level and experience, mana and movement points
  • Secondary skills, army slots, backpack artifacts and spells
  • Player resources: gold, wood, ore, mercury, sulfur, crystals and gems
  • Restoration of Erathia, Armageddon's Blade, Shadow of Death (Complete), Heroes Chronicles, HD Edition and In the Wake of Gods saves

Web successor of Heroes III SaveEditor 2.05 (hmm3se.exe) by VItonIC Group, the Windows editor from 2001–2005.
